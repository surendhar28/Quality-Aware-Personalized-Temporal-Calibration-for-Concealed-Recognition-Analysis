# GazeGuard AI — Measurement Limitations, Confounders, and Provenance Boundaries (Revision 12)

## 1. Participant and Recording Session Provenance

### The 39-Workbook vs. 38-Participant Mapping
- The physical Zenodo repository (`10.5281/zenodo.18525952`) contains **39 Excel recording workbooks** named `CIT Recording1.xlsx` through `CIT Recording40.xlsx` (omitting recording index #14).
- Direct workbook inspection reveals that `CIT Recording25.xlsx` and `CIT Recording27.xlsx` both record the same participant identifier (`Participant name: Participant25`, group: `guilty`, gender: `Female`).
- Consequently, the derived feature table comprises **38 unique biological participants** (20 guilty, 18 control).
- Researchers must account for this multi-session mapping: evaluating models with participant-grouped cross-validation (`StratifiedGroupKFold`) grouping strictly on `participant_id` (not file name) is mandatory to prevent cross-validation data leakage.

## 2. Target Variable and Supervised Label Limitations

> [!CAUTION]
> **Supervised Target Caveat:**
> In the evaluated derived dataset, the supervised target `familiarity_label` is an experimental condition label assigned by the mock-crime protocol.
> - The label is **constant across all trials** for a given participant (1 for all trials of a guilty participant, 0 for all trials of a control participant).
> - Predictions reflect condition-level variance across participants rather than trial-by-trial physiological recognition of individual stimulus items.
> - **Predictions must never be interpreted as evidence of deception, guilt, intent, or dishonesty.**

---

## 3. Raw Data vs. Derived Feature Provenance Limitations

- **Upstream Fixation Parsing:** Features in `data/processed/zenodo_features.csv` (such as `early_probe_diff`, `mean_fix_duration`, `fixation_count`) depend on upstream fixation events generated by Tobii Studio / Tobii Pro Lab software utilizing an I-VT (Velocity-Threshold Identification) filter.
- **Unverified Filter Hyperparameters:** Exact velocity thresholds, minimum fixation duration cutoffs, and windowing boundaries configured during initial Tobii export were pre-baked into the Excel exports.
- **Pending Direct Raw Verification:** Direct derivation of all 11 features from raw coordinate streams $(x, y, t)$ across all 39 workbooks remains pending Phase 2 complete feature provenance validation.

## 4. Hardware and Measurement Differences

| Parameter | Tobii Pro Fusion (Evaluated Benchmark) | Browser Webcam (Demonstration Mode) |
|---|---|---|
| **Sampling Rate** | 120–250 Hz | 30–60 Hz (browser throttled) |
| **Spatial Precision** | ~0.4° – 0.8° visual angle (~15–30 px) | ~1.5° – 3.5° visual angle (~60–140 px) |
| **Illumination Robustness** | Active near-infrared illuminators | Ambient visible RGB (sensitive to shadows/glare) |
| **Head Motion Tolerance** | 3D head-box tracking with parallax compensation | Highly sensitive to head yaw, pitch, roll, and distance |
| **Timestamp Precision** | Sub-millisecond hardware clock | Browser JavaScript event-loop timing jitter |

> [!WARNING]
> Models trained on high-precision infrared hardware data **cannot be assumed to generalize or transfer to webcam eye-tracking**. Webcam demonstrations in the application are exploratory proof-of-concept interfaces only.

---

## 5. Psychophysiological Confounders & Generalizability

1. **Visual Saliency vs. Recognition:** Low-level visual properties (contrast, luminance, visual complexity, edge density) can attract automatic visual orienting during the initial 500 ms regardless of memory.
2. **Fatigue and Saccadic Slowing:** Repeated trial presentations induce cognitive fatigue, saccadic slowing, and increased blink artifacts.
3. **Cohort Boundaries:** The evaluated sample comprises 38 young adult university students and staff in Poland. Results cannot be generalized across elderly cohorts, clinical populations, individuals with corrective eyewear, or high-stakes field environments.
